A companion for every trip day
• On a trip day, Loci opens on today: the city, the day and your next stop.
• Optional: hear about saved places nearby that fit your taste.
• Siri: "Plan a day in Loci" and "What's near me that I'd like in Loci".

Trip journal
• Notes and photos for each day, with stops ticked off as you visit them.
• Visit logging is optional and off until you turn it on.
• Play a finished trip back as a story, and share it as an image or a link.

Your taste
• Mark places you've been, say "Not for me", and tell Loci how a place was.
• See how well a place matches you and why, and keep top-10 lists.
• Import places into a list from a TikTok, YouTube, Google Maps or blog link.

Plan and share
• Trip Plan: dates, a hotel per city, flight searches, and Ask the planner.
• Invite friends from Profile with a link that doesn't expire; find friends from your contacts.
• Leaderboard, progress and streaks.
• Today's destination: a daily city pick at the top of Boards, labelled as AI generated.

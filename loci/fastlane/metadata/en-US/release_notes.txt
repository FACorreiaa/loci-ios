Plans that work for your access needs
• Tell Loci how you get around in your travel profile: mobility, longest walk between stops, step-free routes, slopes, cobblestones, rest stops, accessible toilets, and sensory needs.
• Itineraries respect those limits. Stops show access notes marked Barrier, Caution or Good to know, each labelled with where it comes from.
• The walk between stops shows its time, and Loci suggests transit or a taxi when a walk is too long.
• Storing access needs is off until you turn it on, and you can delete them at any time.

Find flights
• Search flights to a city from your results or your trip plan, with airport suggestions as you type.
• Loci opens the search on a flight site, where the prices and booking are. Save the flight to your trip.
